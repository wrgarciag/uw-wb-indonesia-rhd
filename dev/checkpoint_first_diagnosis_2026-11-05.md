## 6. Why adult deaths changed without lowering D mortality

- **Indonesia (worse).** Removing direct C/D entry and the A→B transfer cut D inflow. But GBD childhood deaths (821/yr; childhood case fatality 0.63% vs adult 0.41%) can now only arise from A→B→C→D within childhood. The optimiser therefore drove A→B and C→D to their upper bounds (0.25 / 0.30).
  - Because B→C and C→D are shared with adults, adult C→D inflow became about 24,100/yr (2019) and D occupancy 16.7% (before 11.2%; GBD HF proxy 3.2%). Realised D fatality is 0.1175 vs the 0.119 input. Childhood deaths still reach only 0.36–0.50×.
- **Uganda (better).** Removing direct D entry (≈1,580/yr before) cut deaths from 1,522 to 672 (2019). D share fell from 5.1% to 2.1% with B→C at 0.0038. Incidence α 0.46–0.78 is still used to hold deaths down, which keeps prevalence at 0.74–0.78×.

## 9. Limiting mechanisms (why fit fails under the fixed assumptions)

1. **Shared progression vs childhood case fatality (Indonesia).** A-only childhood entry plus one B→C/C→D at all ages cannot give GBD's childhood deaths without inflating adult deaths.
2. **Prior-A exclusion at 21.** Child A counts as RHD but the same lesion stops counting at 21. Prevalence at 20–24 is 0.45–0.74× in both countries, against GBD's young-adult prevalence peak.
3. **Too few adult exits (Uganda).** Only B normalisation 0.02 plus progression. GBD prevalence falls steeply with age, so the model is too low at 20–44 and too high at 60+.
4. **GBD case-fatality trend 2010–2019.** IDN prevalence +11% while deaths −13%, so deaths per prevalent case fall 21% (childhood deaths −27%). UGA deaths per prevalent case fall 14%. Constant natural history plus flat reference care cannot reproduce this, so model/target death ratios rise about 25–35% across the scored years. That drift alone consumes most of the ±0.25 band.

## 11. Decision needed (asked in the session)

A credible fit needs a change to a fixed assumption. Options:

1. Free three attained-age adult parameters (adult B→C, C→D, B→normalised), **and** count adult prior-A in the GBD prevalence mapping. This keeps the A definition consistent across age 21. Expected: prevalence within tolerance; deaths still drift above tolerance in later years.
2. Free only the three adult parameters and keep prior-A excluded.
3. Keep the specification as is; accept FAIL (05–08 not updated, or updated only with an INADEQUATE label).

Separately, passing the death tolerance in *every* scored year would need either a historical case-fatality / care trend or a different scoring rule. Both are decisions, not fixes.
